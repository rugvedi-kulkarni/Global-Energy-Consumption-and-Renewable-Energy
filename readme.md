# 🌍 Global Energy Consumption and Renewable Energy Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on exploring global energy consumption and renewable energy trends using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.

It analyzes energy consumption patterns, compares renewable and non-renewable energy sources, and explores changes in global energy production through data visualization.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze global energy consumption over time.
- Compare renewable and non-renewable energy sources.
- Explore solar and wind energy production.
- Compare energy consumption across countries.
- Identify global energy trends and patterns.
- Understand the growth of renewable energy.

## 📂 Dataset

- **Dataset:** Our World in Data – Energy Dataset
- **Source:** [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/energy)
- **Domain:** Global Energy and Renewable Energy

The dataset contains information related to energy consumption, fossil fuels, renewable energy, electricity generation, and country-wise energy statistics.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Concepts Applied

- Data loading and manipulation using Pandas.
- Data filtering and grouping.
- Line charts and bar charts.
- Scatter plots and histograms.
- Area and stacked charts.
- Figure customization and multiple subplots.
- Country-wise and year-wise comparisons.

## 🎓 Learning Outcomes

- Practical experience with Matplotlib.
- Working with real-world energy datasets.
- Understanding global energy trends.
- Selecting suitable visualization techniques.
- Presenting analytical observations through graphs.
